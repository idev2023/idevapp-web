iDev News Ticker

A notice bar at the top of every page, fixed or scrolling at 3 speeds, with start and end times, links, colours and Thai / English text.

  • Version 1.0.0
  • WordPress 6.2+
  • PHP 7.4+
  • Language Thai / English

1Features

  • Fixed or scrolling slow / normal / fast
  • Pauses on hover, and doesn’t scroll if the visitor prefers reduced motion
  • Set start / end times in advance; several notices at once
  • A label such as "Important", text and link in Thai / English
  • Placed automatically at the top of every page, or wherever you put the shortcode

2Getting started

  1. Add a notice

    Notice bar › Add a notice Enter the label, text and link, and choose the display and dates

  2. Set the colours and position

    iDevApp › Notice bar settings Position Automatic (top of the page) or Manual background, text and label colours, and content width

To pause a notice, switch it to Draft · automatic placement works with themes that support wp_body_open (most themes)

3Shortcodes and developers

Shortcode / functionResult
[idev_ticker]Shows the notice bar here (set the position to "Manual")
idev_ticker_render()call it from a theme file

Themes that place the bar themselves declare add_theme_support('idev-news-ticker') and the plugin won’t add it twice
