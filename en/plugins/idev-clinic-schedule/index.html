iDev Clinic Schedule

Manage doctors and clinics with weekly, monthly, yearly or one-off clinic hours, shown as month / week / day schedules, a doctors section with tabs by speciality, and an "Open now · closes 20:00" status from your opening hours.

  • WordPress 6.2+
  • PHP 7.4+

1Features

  • Clinic hours: Every week · every month (e.g. the 1st and 3rd Wednesday, or the 15th) · every year · on a date with an optional date range
  • Shortcuts ＋ Mon–Fri / ＋ Sat–Sun · template common times · copy hours from another doctor
  • Views: Month / week / day filter by doctor / clinic / speciality, and click a time to book
  • Doctors with photos, speciality and biography, in tabs by speciality
  • Opening hours and a live open / closed status, including closing days
  • Holidays from iDev Thai Holidays replace clinic hours automatically

2Getting started

  1. Add a doctor or clinic

    Doctors and clinics › Add doctor / clinic Enter the name (e.g. Dr Somchai Jaidee), choose the type Doctor or Clinic / department English name, speciality, note, biography and featured image

  2. Add clinic hours

    Click ＋ Add time choose the pattern, the day and the start–end time, or use the Mon–Fri shortcut. If several doctors share the same hours, click Save as template and use it for the others

  3. Group specialities into tabs

    The Speciality box on the right of the edit screen; a doctor can be in several. The tab order is set on the speciality edit screen (lower numbers first).

  4. Set opening hours and the booking link

    Doctors and clinics › Settings Enter each day’s opening hours, the phone number, the default view, colours and the booking link (with iDev Appointments it is linked for you)

  5. Put the schedule on a page

    Create a page (suggested slug doctors) with the block Clinic schedule and Doctors or a shortcode below

To hide a doctor for a while, switch them to Draft · ordering: Attributes → Order

3Blocks and shortcodes

ShortcodeWhat it does
[idev_schedule]Schedule with month / week / day buttons, filters and date navigation
[idev_schedule view="day" navigation="0"]Today only, no buttons (good for the home page)
[idev_schedule kind="clinic" specialty="pediatrics"]Filter by type or speciality (the speciality slug)
[idev_doctors]Doctors in tabs by speciality
[idev_doctors kind="all" columns="4" tabs="0" bio="0"]Options: kind doctor|clinic|all · columns · tabs · bio · schedule_link · specialty
[idev_open_status]"Open now · closes 20:00" status

All three are also blocks in the iDevApp category of the page editor: Clinic schedule · Doctors · Open / closed status

4Troubleshooting

Times don’t appear in the schedule

  • The time row is incomplete (the end must be after the start, and a day / date must be chosen); incomplete rows aren’t saved
  • The date range hasn’t started yet or has ended
  • The doctor is a draft

The open / closed status is wrong

Check Timezone in Settings › General is set to Bangkok, and check the opening hours in the settings
