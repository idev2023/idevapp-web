I-Work · GPS time attendance and work reports

Reliable clock-in from a phone, no queue at the scanner

Staff clock in and out on their phones. The system checks their location against the clock-in point and asks for a photo. HR gets the hours straight away, with nothing to collect by hand.

  • GPS is checked against each clock-in point and its radius. Outside the area you choose whether staff give a reason (field work, home visits) or cannot clock in.
  • Photos come only from the camera on the clock-in page; old or repeated photos are rejected
  • Detects fake-location apps, jumping coordinates and wrong device clocks
  • Works with ZKTeco face scanners: enrol once, scan on any device at any branch, all in the same report as GPS clock-ins
  • Each person gets their attendance summary by email and LINE OA and can check their own hours without asking HR
  • Rosters, overnight shifts, late / early / absent summaries and time corrections through an approval chain, with Excel export
  • Set how long photos and locations are kept under the PDPA; they are deleted automatically

I-SOS · Emergency alert system

One button to raise an alert, teams know at once, follow it to the end

From a staff member asking for help on their phone, to announcing an incident across the organisation, to dispatching and tracking vehicles from the command centre: everyone involved is alerted at the same time and sees the same status.

Command centre

Incident and evacuation control room

During an evacuation the system uses the latest I-Work clock-ins to find who may still be in the area. Everyone confirms they are safe on their phone, and the controller sees the overview and a list sorted by urgency.

  • Fire, flood, earthquake, chemical leak, violence and evacuation
  • People asking for help appear at the top with their latest GPS location and a map link
  • The controller can mark people "found / safe"; the screen refreshes every 15 seconds
  • Print the list, and keep a record of every incident to review after drills

I-Queue · Service queue system

Follow every queue on LINE instead of waiting outside the room

Patients type their queue number into your LINE account once. The system follows them from screening to the doctor to the pharmacy and alerts them as their turn approaches, so there are fewer crowds outside the exam rooms and pharmacy.

  • Runs on your LINE Official Account; patients don’t install anything
  • Register once with the 13-digit ID number; after that just type the queue number
  • One message follows every step: screening, the doctor and the pharmacy
  • Automatic alerts at 10 and 5 people ahead and when it’s your turn, saying where to go
  • Colour-coded alert cards that are easy to read, even for older patients
  • Queues on screens at each service point, and a dashboard for administrators

I-Office FAQ

Questions? Here are the answers

Where is our data stored?

All data stays on your own server, not on the developer’s cloud. The database is backed up automatically every night and kept for 30 days.

What kind of server do we need?

Windows 10/11, Windows Server 2016 or later, or Linux (AlmaLinux / Rocky / RHEL, or Docker). The system uses Node.js and PostgreSQL, which the install script sets up for you.

Can we move our data from Excel or an old program?

Yes. In the import menu, download the templates for departments, assets, users and parts, fill them in and upload them. Every row is checked and previewed before it is saved.

Can we use it outside the office or on a phone?

It works in any phone browser. For access from outside, set up a Cloudflare Tunnel with the script provided; no firewall ports need opening.

What happens when the licence expires?

Everything keeps working and all your data stays. You just can’t update to new versions until you renew.

How does GPS attendance stop people clocking in for each other?

A photo must be taken with the camera on the clock-in page every time; old or repeated photos are rejected. The location is checked against the clock-in point, and fake-location apps, jumping coordinates and wrong device clocks are detected. Suspicious entries are flagged for the supervisor.

Can we see the other systems too?
