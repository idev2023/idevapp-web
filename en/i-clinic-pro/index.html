Prescribe from stock, re-med in one click

Type or scan a barcode and the drug goes on the bill with its directions for the label. If a patient allergic to penicillin is prescribed amoxicillin, a red warning must be confirmed first.

Frequently asked questions

Before you decide

Do we need internet all the time?

It is a web system used in the browser, installed on hosting we arrange (so it works anywhere). If you would like it on a computer in the clinic running on your local network, just ask.

Can we use any number of computers and users?

Yes. There is no charge per computer or per user. Doctors, nurses and cashiers have separate permissions, and doctors can use a tablet or phone in the exam room.

We have drugs and patients in Excel. Can we move them?

Yes. Drug lists import straight from CSV / Excel, and we help format the files during installation.

Is patient data safe?

Treatment records, addresses and ID numbers are stored encrypted, users have separate permissions, every visit records who did what, and backups are set up during installation.

Is there anything to pay after the first year?

The system keeps working with nothing more to pay. Hosting / domain from the second year are charged at cost, and you can take a yearly support contract if you want continued support.

What is the difference between I-Clinic and I-Clinic Pro?

I-Clinic (22,900 THB) has everything except custom orders · I-Clinic Pro adds job tickets, prescriptions / job details, deposits and pick-up appointments, for clinics selling glasses, lenses or hearing aids or using labs.
